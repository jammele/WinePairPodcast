Do not repeat: one character defensively blocking an object thrust at their face by a third, off-camera limb, while the other character reacts as an amused bystander rather than the one delivering the action.

Do not repeat: one character theatrically overheated and self-cooling with a chilled glass while the other character sips serenely, visibly unbothered by the same heat.
