## Recent scenes (do not repeat these physical actions)

- **Ep218 (approved):** Joe is throwing both hands up in gleeful surrender as a tumbling crowd of small bottles labeled "MALVASIA" spills toward the viewer from below. Carmela is trying to count the bottles on her fingers, already out of fingers, laughing and glancing at Joe. Do not repeat: both characters reacting to a flood or overflow of multiple arriving objects from below frame.

- **Ep219 (approved):** Joe is pressing both hands flat onto a surface on either side of a bottle labeled "CHARLES SHAW CHARDONNAY," leaning forward with intense deliberative weight. Carmela is resting her chin on her hand, staring at the same bottle with sharp theatrical concentration. Neither character looks at the other. Do not repeat: two static characters staring at one central object on a surface between them, both still, neither physically acting on it.

- **Ep221 (approved):** Joe is stacking enormous history books one-handed into a dangerously top-heavy wobbling tower (spines: "7,000 YEARS," "PHOENICIA," "CANA") while balancing a bottle labeled "LEBANESE WINE" on top, face in trembling concentration, tongue slightly out. Carmela is flinching backward with arms half-raised, alarmed eyes, delighted panic — she can see exactly what is about to happen. Do not repeat: one character precariously stacking or balancing multiple objects into an unstable tower while balancing a prop on top, while the other reacts with alarm and anticipatory panic.

- **Ep222 (approved):** Joe is jabbing his finger emphatically at a honeycomb dripping amber honey into a wine glass, mouth wide open mid-word, face urgent and earnest — talking about the wine. Carmela is pressing both hands flat over her heart, eyes closed, full swooning smile — she thinks Joe called her "honey" and is overwhelmed by the compliment. A bottle labeled "ALSACE PINOT GRIS" stands beside the honeycomb. Do not repeat: one character excitedly pointing at a food or flavor prop while the other character completely misreads the gesture as a personal endearment and reacts emotionally.

- **Ep224 (approved):** Joe is hunched over a clipboard, scribbling tasting notes with exaggerated seriousness while trying to keep a straight face. Carmela is popping a tight burst of confetti directly over a bottle labeled "MOSCATO D'ASTI," grinning like she just exposed the guilty-pleasure truth. A small tulip-shaped white wine glass sits beside the bottle. Do not repeat: one character performing fake-formal note-taking while the other punctures the seriousness with a controlled celebratory action over the central bottle.
