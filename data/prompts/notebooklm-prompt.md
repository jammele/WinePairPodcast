NotebookLM Prompt

I am looking for articles and information on the wine XXXXX. Please focus on information that contains the following: what the wine is, information about its background and history, how it is made, where it is made, the reputation of the wine, sales and exports of the wine, and any interesting controversies about the wine. Please focus on links from sources that are credible, valid, objective, and are not behind paywalls. Avoid any links that lead to error pages or 404s. Please do not bring back any links from Reddit or from specific wineries or wine brands. All sources should be objective 3rd party journalistic, academic, and/or expert sources. 



FOR CONTENT
Can you give me the history of XXXX? I am looking for where it is from, the history behind it, where the name comes from, how it is made, and what it’s reputation is today. 




REUSABLE NOTEBOOKLM RESEARCH PROMPT FOR THE WINE PAIR PODCAST
================================================================

I need research sources for an upcoming episode about [WINE NAME/TOPIC].

Please find approximately 20 credible sources (articles, videos, educational content) that I can add to NotebookLM.

REQUIREMENTS:
✅ Focus on: what the wine is, background/history, how it's made, where it's made, reputation, sales/exports, interesting controversies
✅ Credible sources: journalistic, academic, expert wine education sites
✅ Objective third-party sources only (no winery marketing, no Reddit)
✅ Not behind paywalls
✅ Working URLs that NotebookLM can access

AVOID:
❌ Wine Folly links (they don't work in NotebookLM)
❌ Meininger's International links
❌ Reddit threads
❌ Individual winery websites or brand marketing
❌ Paywalled content
❌ Error pages or 404s

DELIVERABLES:
1. A detailed document with descriptions of each source and why it's useful
2. A separate clean list of JUST the URLs (no numbers, no descriptions) that I can copy-paste directly into NotebookLM and into my show notes

YouTube videos: Please provide search terms for finding quality educational videos, as direct YouTube links sometimes don't work.

================================================================
EXAMPLE USAGE:

"I need research sources for an upcoming episode about Albariño."

OR

"I need research sources for an upcoming episode about Bordeaux blanc."

OR

"I need research sources for an upcoming episode about orange wine."
