Wine in the News Prompt

Act as a researcher and scriptwriter for "The Wine Pair Podcast." I need you to find 3 distinct wine-related news stories published in the last 14 days that we can use for our "Wine in the News" segment.

Search Criteria:
1. Topic: Focus on stories that are surprising, quirky, controversial, dramatic, or funny. Look for scandals, crimes, lawsuits, bizarre regulations, weird science, or "wait, what?!" moments.
2. Exclude: Wine reviews, tasting notes, generic harvest updates, boring financial reports, or event listings.
3. Sources: Use only credible journalistic or industry sources that are free-to-read (no hard paywalls). All links must be live and valid.

Drafting Instructions:
Once you find the stories, please draft a podcast script for ONE of them that I can read on air. Use the following strict style guide:

* Structure:
    1.  Start by citing the specific article title, author, and publication name.
    2.  Use the exact phrase: "The short story is this:" followed by a one-sentence summary.
    3.  Transition to the details using the phrase: "For more context..." or "Here is the scoop:"
    4.  End with a question I can ask my co-host, Carmela.
* Tone: Conversational, narrative, and factual. Explain *why* a famous figure or winery is relevant to a casual listener (e.g., "Listeners might know this brand because...").
* Punctuation Constraint: Do NOT use em-dashes (—). Replace them with commas or new sentences.
* Factuality: Do not invent details or offer conjecture. Stick strictly to the facts in the source.

Please present the 3 story options first with headlines and links, and then provide the full draft for the best one.
