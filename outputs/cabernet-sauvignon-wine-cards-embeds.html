Malbec

Mendoza, Argentina
Bold and Fruit-Forward

Argentina's flagship red: dark fruit, smooth tannins, and everyday drinkability at a price that overdelivers every time. Blueberry and chocolate where Cab has blackberry and cedar, with less grip and more immediate pleasure. The first stop for anyone who loves Cab but wants something a little easier.

Piedmont, Italy
Classic and Food-Friendly

Zinfandel

California, USA
Bold and Fruit-Forward

California's bold red: jammy blackberry fruit, high alcohol, and a spice note (cinnamon, black pepper, mocha) that runs from front to back. Less structured tannin than Cab but the same weight and the same instinct for red meat and BBQ. The American answer to the question of what a bold red should taste like.
