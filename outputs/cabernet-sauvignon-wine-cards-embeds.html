Cabernet Franc

Loire Valley, France / New World
Classic and Food-Friendly

Cabernet Sauvignon's parent grape: same dark cherry fruit with a herbal, violet edge and lighter tannin. Black cherry, bell pepper, graphite, with higher acidity and more flexibility at the table than its offspring. More elegant than powerful, and one of the best food wines on this list.
