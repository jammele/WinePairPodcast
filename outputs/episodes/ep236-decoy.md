**Q. Is Decoy wine worth buying?**
A. Decoy is a safe, solid mass-market choice rather than a must-buy. The 2023 California Cabernet Sauvignon ($19.97 paid at wine.com) and 2025 Sauvignon Blanc ($16.97 paid) each scored 6/10 from both hosts, who would drink them but probably wouldn't buy them for themselves. One host called it a great buy for a large group.

**Q. What is the difference between Decoy and Duckhorn wines?**
A. Decoy is the lower-priced companion label to Duckhorn Vineyards, launched in 1985 and positioned as the "gateway duck" toward the Duckhorn family. Decoy uses fruit from vineyards across California and is made to be ready on release, while Duckhorn Vineyards centers on Napa Valley estate vineyards and can cost $60 or more.

**Q. What does Decoy Cabernet Sauvignon taste like?**
A. The 2023 Decoy California Cabernet Sauvignon is dark purple with cherry, black currant, cinnamon and smoke, a tart edge, noticeable acidity and grippy tannins. One host found it aggressive and lacking subtlety; the other found it less punchy than expected and a little flat. Both rated it 6/10.

**Q. Does a wine labeled Cabernet Sauvignon have to be 100% Cabernet Sauvignon?**
A. No. Cabernet Sauvignon on the label does not mean 100% Cabernet Sauvignon. The 2023 Decoy California Cabernet Sauvignon contains 76% Cabernet Sauvignon and 24% Merlot.

**Q. What does Decoy Sauvignon Blanc taste like?**
A. The 2025 Decoy California Sauvignon Blanc is 100% Sauvignon Blanc at 13.9% alcohol, with tropical fruit, ripe pear and lemon aromas and a creamy note. The palate is creamy but sharp and steely, with a hard-alcohol edge the hosts likened to a lemon drop or vodka. Both hosts rated it 6/10.
