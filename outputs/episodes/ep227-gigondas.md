## COVER ART

### Ep227 — Gigondas Has a Reputation for Punching You in the Face. Does It Live Up to That?

**Concept history:** Originally "Corner Prep" (Joe gloving up while Carmela laces him, no clear cause for the gloves). Revised twice on pose mechanics (raised single fist kept misreading as a victory pose). Then reconceived entirely: Joe rightly flagged that the title is about the *wine* having a punch-you-in-the-face reputation, not Joe becoming a boxer, and that the gloves needed a visible reason to be on. Final concept, "The Jab": an off-camera mystery arm thrusts a wine glass at Joe's face like a jab; Joe blocks with both gloves; Carmela is repositioned as an amused bystander (not the attacker, to avoid any spousal-conflict read).
